Predicting Stock Price Volatility

Student Researcher · Advisor: Prof. H. Mete Soner (Princeton University)

An end-to-end pipeline for stock-price volatility prediction, using CNN for local temporal patterns, BiLSTM for long-range dependencies, and an attention mechanism, evaluated against baseline models and compiled into a published academic paper.
